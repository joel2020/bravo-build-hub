Every Westchester HVAC quote includes warranty language that's intentionally confusing. Here's what each type actually covers and what's worth your money.

## The 3 layers of HVAC warranty

### 1. Manufacturer parts warranty (comes with equipment)

- **Standard:** 5–10 years on parts (compressor, heat exchanger usually 10)
- **Registration required:** Most manufacturers require online registration within 60–90 days of install or you drop to 5 years
- **Doesn't cover:** Labor, refrigerant, diagnostic time
- **Doesn't cover:** Damage from improper install, lack of maintenance, surge damage

### 2. Installer labor warranty (varies by contractor)

- **Bravo standard:** 2 years labor on installs
- **Industry range:** 30 days (cheap installers) to 10 years (premium installers)
- **What it covers:** The labor cost to diagnose and replace a failed warranty part — manufacturer covers the part, installer covers the time

### 3. Extended service plans (sold separately)

- **Sold by manufacturers:** typically $400–$1,200 to extend labor coverage 10 years
- **Sold by contractors:** annual maintenance + parts/labor coverage
- **Sold by third parties:** like a Choice Home Warranty — generally not worth it for HVAC

## What voids your warranty (the fine print they hide)

- **Skipping annual maintenance** — most manufacturers require documented annual service
- **Using non-OEM parts** — even if cheaper
- **Operating outside design conditions** — running AC below 60°F outdoor temp, etc.
- **Improper install** — no Manual J, oversized equipment, bad refrigerant charge
- **Not registering within the window** — automatic warranty downgrade

## Warranty registration — do this immediately after install

A reputable installer registers your warranty for you. Confirm in writing. If they refuse, register yourself within 60 days at the manufacturer's site (Carrier, Trane, Mitsubishi, Daikin, etc.).

## What we recommend for Westchester homeowners

**Budget builds:** Take the standard manufacturer warranty + installer labor warranty. Skip extended.

**Mid-range or new construction:** Buy the manufacturer's 10-year extended labor warranty. Often $600–$1,200 and covers what would otherwise be $1,500–$4,000 in labor over the life of the system.

**Heat pumps and high-efficiency systems:** Definitely get the extended labor warranty. These have more electronic components that can fail outside the standard labor window.

## Warranty red flags when comparing quotes

- **30-day labor warranty** — they don't trust their own work
- **No written warranty** — only "we stand behind it" verbally
- **"Lifetime warranty" with no fine print** — almost always either tied to maintenance contract or extremely limited
- **Refusal to register manufacturer warranty for you** — laziness or worse
