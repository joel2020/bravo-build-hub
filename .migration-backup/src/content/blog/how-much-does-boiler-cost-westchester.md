**Quick answer:** Boiler replacement cost in Westchester County depends on the equipment type, fuel source, venting, piping, home access, code requirements, and whether related work is needed. The ranges below are planning estimates only. A final quote requires an on-site assessment.

**Important pricing note:** Pricing and incentive availability vary by equipment, home conditions, utility program rules, code requirements, and current market costs. This guide is for planning only; final pricing requires an on-site assessment.

## Planning price ranges

| System | Planning range | Typical lifespan |
|---|---|---|
| Cast-iron gas boiler | $7,500–$11,500+ | 20–30 yrs |
| High-efficiency condensing gas boiler | $9,500–$15,000+ | 15–20 yrs |
| Combi boiler (heat + hot water) | $10,500–$16,500+ | 15–20 yrs |
| Oil boiler replacement | $9,000–$14,000+ | 20–25 yrs |
| Oil-to-gas conversion | $14,000–$22,000+ | 20–25 yrs |
| Electric boiler | $4,500–$8,500+ | 15–20 yrs |

These ranges assume a typical residential replacement with reasonable access. Older Westchester homes can require additional work before a boiler replacement can be completed safely and correctly.

## What can drive the price up in Westchester

- **Venting or chimney work** — may be needed when equipment type, fuel source, or venting requirements change
- **Asbestos or legacy insulation concerns** — sometimes present around older mechanical rooms and piping
- **Near-boiler piping corrections** — especially on older steam or hydronic systems
- **Permit and inspection requirements** — vary by municipality and project scope
- **Gas line, electrical, or control updates** — may be required for some high-efficiency or combi systems
- **Access and removal difficulty** — tight basements, stairs, and old equipment can increase labor

## Incentives and rebates

Some homeowners may qualify for utility, state, or federal incentives depending on equipment type, efficiency rating, program rules, and installation details. Program availability and eligibility can change, so confirm current requirements before making a purchase decision.

If a heat pump or hybrid system is being considered as an alternative to boiler replacement, incentive options may be different from a like-for-like boiler project.

## Westchester-specific pricing notes

- **Yonkers, Mount Vernon, New Rochelle:** older homes may need venting, piping, or abatement considerations before replacement
- **Scarsdale, Bronxville, Larchmont:** larger homes and tight mechanical rooms can make design and access more important
- **Northern Westchester:** propane, oil, and larger homes can change equipment selection and installation scope

## Should you replace or repair?

Replacement may be worth discussing if your boiler is:

- Near the end of its expected service life
- Leaking or showing signs of section failure
- Requiring repeated seasonal repairs
- Struggling to heat the home evenly
- Paired with outdated controls or failing piping

Repair may still make sense if the issue is isolated, parts are available, and the boiler is otherwise operating safely.
