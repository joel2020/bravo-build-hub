# Local landing-page evidence review - 2026-09-02

## Review method

All material locality claims were checked against primary government sources. New York State's Westchester municipality inventory and Local Government Handbook support city, town, village, hamlet, coterminous-government, and parent-municipality statements. Route-specific municipal sources were used for Yonkers, Chappaqua, Bedford, Katonah, Armonk, Briarcliff Manor, and Hartsdale. NYSERDA supports the system-configuration context, and the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission supports carbon-monoxide safety boundaries.

The review does not treat postal place names, ZIP Codes, neighborhoods, or nearby-city lists as municipal boundaries. Permit language tells the reader to verify current requirements and responsibilities; it does not claim that Bravo Mechanical files, coordinates, or closes permits.

## ZIP and neighborhood handling

The seeded Yonkers value 10701 was removed instead of being expanded from secondary ZIP aggregators. No authoritative source reviewed established an exhaustive Yonkers service-boundary ZIP list, and several Westchester postal names overlap municipal boundaries. All zips and neighborhoods arrays are therefore intentionally empty in this evidence-reviewed layer. This prevents a partial list from being rendered or interpreted as exhaustive; the route and written address/jurisdiction guidance carry the service-area context.
