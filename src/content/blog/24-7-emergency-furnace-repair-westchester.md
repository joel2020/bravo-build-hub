## Step 1: Check these 4 things before you call (5 minutes)

Most "no heat" calls have a homeowner-fixable cause:

1. **Thermostat** — set to HEAT, temp 5°F above current room temp, batteries fresh
2. **Furnace switch** — looks like a light switch on the side of the unit. Should be ON.
3. **Breaker** — flip OFF then back ON in the main panel
4. **Power reset** — switch furnace off for 60 seconds, then back on

If none of those fixes it, call us.

## Step 2: While you wait — keep the house safe

- **Set thermostat to 62°F minimum** to prevent pipe freeze (open cabinets under sinks)
- **Trickle one cold faucet** on the highest floor with exterior plumbing
- **Use space heaters safely:** plugged direct into the wall (no extension cord), 3 ft clearance, off when sleeping
- **Never use a stove, oven, or grill for heat** — CO poisoning kills
- **Test CO alarms** — battery and function

## When it's a 911 call, not an HVAC call

Call **911 immediately** if:

- CO detector is alarming
- You smell gas (rotten eggs)
- Visible flames outside the burner box
- You have flu-like symptoms that improve when you leave the house
