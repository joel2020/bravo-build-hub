**Quick answer:** In Westchester County, NY, typical HVAC equipment lifespans are: **gas furnace 15–20 years**, **cast-iron boiler 25–30 years**, **central AC 12–15 years**, **heat pump 12–15 years**, **ductless mini-split 15–20 years**, **tank water heater 8–12 years**, **tankless water heater 18–22 years**. Sound Shore (Rye, Mamaroneck, Larchmont) AC condensers can lose 2–3 years to salt-air corrosion.

| Equipment | Manufacturer claim | Westchester reality | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cast-iron gas/oil boiler | 30+ yrs | 25–30 yrs | Hard water scaling |
| High-efficiency condensing boiler | 20 yrs | 15–18 yrs | Acidic condensate |
| Gas furnace | 20 yrs | 15–20 yrs | Filter neglect drops it 3–5 yrs |

## What kills HVAC equipment in Westchester

1. **Hard water** (140–180 ppm in most of Westchester) — scales heat exchangers, water heaters, combi boilers
2. **Salt air** within 1 mile of the Sound — corrodes condenser coils 2–3× faster
3. **Heavy oak/maple pollen** April–June — clogs outdoor coils
4. **Long heating season** (Oct–April) — more cycles than warmer climates
5. **Filter neglect** — single biggest controllable lifespan factor

## How to add 3–5 years to any system

- Replace 1" filters every 60–90 days
- Annual professional tune-up (combustion analysis, not a "free check")
- Wash outdoor condenser coil every spring
- Flush tankless water heater annually
- Keep 3 ft clear around outdoor units
- Address minor repairs immediately — small leaks become big ones

## When repair stops making sense

Use the **$5,000 / 50% rule**: if repair cost × age in years > $5,000, or if a single repair > 50% of replacement cost, replace.

Example: 14-year-old AC, $1,800 compressor repair → 14 × 1,800 = $25,200 → replace. Or: 12-yr boiler, $4,500 heat exchanger → > 50% of replacement → replace.

## Westchester-specific lifespan tips by region

- **Sound Shore (Rye, Mamaroneck, Larchmont):** Always upgrade to salt-air-coated condenser ($300–$600 extra; pays for itself).
- **Northern Westchester (Bedford, Katonah, Yorktown):** Long propane runs — annual gas-line inspection prevents costly leaks.
- **Lower Westchester (Yonkers, Mount Vernon):** Older homes — check chimney lining when replacing any combustion equipment.
- **Rivertowns (Tarrytown, Irvington):** High humidity basements — coil mold shortens AC life; upgrade filtration.
