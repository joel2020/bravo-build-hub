Westchester winters are unforgiving. Lows in the teens, sudden cold snaps, and decades-old housing stock mean your furnace works harder here than in most of the country. A little prep in October or early November can prevent the 2 AM no-heat call in January.

## 1. Replace your air filter

A clogged filter is the #1 cause of furnace short-cycling and blower failures we see across Yonkers, White Plains, and Scarsdale. Swap a 1" filter every 1–3 months during heating season; a 4–5" media filter every 6–12 months.

## 2. Clear the area around the furnace

Keep at least 3 feet of clearance. Stored boxes, paint cans, and laundry detergent near a gas furnace are both a fire hazard and a CO risk.

## 3. Test your thermostat

Switch from cool to heat a week before you need it. If the call for heat doesn't fire the burner within 60 seconds, schedule a service visit before the cold hits.

## 4. Check vents and cold-air returns

Walk the house and confirm every supply register and return grille is open and unblocked by furniture or rugs. Restricted returns drop efficiency and stress the heat exchanger.

## 5. Inspect the flue and intake (high-efficiency systems)

For 90%+ AFUE condensing furnaces, look at the PVC intake and exhaust outside the house. Wasps, leaves, and snow drifts can block them — a blocked intake will lock the furnace out.

## 6. Test your CO detector

Every Westchester home with a gas furnace, boiler, or water heater needs a working CO detector on each level. Press the test button. Replace batteries. Replace the unit itself every 5–7 years.

## 7. Book a professional tune-up

A pro tune-up includes combustion analysis, heat exchanger inspection, gas pressure check, igniter and flame sensor cleaning, and amp-draw readings on the blower. These are the failures that strand families in February.

## When to call us

If you hear banging on startup, smell anything sweet or burning, see soot near vents, or your furnace short-cycles (turns on and off rapidly), shut it down and call. We service Westchester County 24/7.
