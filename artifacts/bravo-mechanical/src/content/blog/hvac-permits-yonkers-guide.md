Yonkers — like every Westchester municipality — requires permits for most HVAC work. Skipping them saves nothing and can cost thousands when you sell.

## When a permit is required in Yonkers

Per the Yonkers Department of Housing & Buildings, you need a permit for:

- New furnace, boiler, or heat pump installation
- AC condenser replacement (yes, even like-for-like)
- New ductwork, gas piping, or oil tank work
- Mini-split installations
- Water heater replacements (gas or electric)

You generally **don't** need a permit for: filter changes, thermostat swaps, capacitor or contactor repairs, or routine maintenance.

## Who can pull the permit

In Yonkers, only a **licensed Westchester County contractor** can pull mechanical, plumbing, or electrical permits. Homeowners cannot self-permit HVAC work in this jurisdiction. If a contractor asks you to pull the permit yourself, that's a red flag.

## What inspections to expect

Most residential HVAC jobs in Yonkers get two inspections:

1. **Rough inspection** — gas piping, venting, electrical, and condensate routing before insulation/closing walls.
2. **Final inspection** — fire-up, combustion analysis, CO test, and verification of the equipment matches the permit.

A final Certificate of Compliance is issued once it passes.

## Why this matters at resale

Westchester real-estate attorneys routinely flag unpermitted mechanical work during title searches. We've seen Yonkers closings delayed 60+ days because a previous owner installed a boiler without a permit. Resolving it after the fact means open inspections, possible re-work to current code, and back-fees.
