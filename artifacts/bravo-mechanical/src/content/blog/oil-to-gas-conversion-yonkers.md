Yonkers has one of the highest concentrations of remaining oil-heat homes in Westchester — particularly in Bryn Mawr, Park Hill, and Crestwood. Converting to natural gas can cut heating costs by **30–45%** and dramatically simplify maintenance. Here's the full process.

## Step 1 — Confirm gas is on your street

Most Yonkers streets have gas mains, but not all. Call Con Edison at 1-800-643-1289 or check their service map. If a main needs to be extended, that can add $5,000–$25,000 (sometimes Con Ed covers it for multi-home conversions).
> Pricing and incentive availability vary by equipment, home conditions, utility program rules, and current market costs. This guide is for planning only; final pricing requires an on-site assessment.


## Step 2 — Get the gas service installed

Con Edison runs the service line from the main to your meter. In Yonkers, this is typically:
- $0–$2,500 for the service line (frequently free for residential conversions)
- 4–10 weeks lead time
- Yonkers Building Department permit required

## Step 3 — Remove the oil tank

- **Above-ground basement tank:** $400–$800 to drain, cut, remove
- **Underground tank:** $1,800–$4,500 (with soil testing)
- **Documentation matters** — Yonkers buyers/insurers will ask for a closure letter

## Step 4 — Install the new gas boiler or furnace

Equipment + install pricing for a typical Yonkers single-family:
- High-efficiency gas boiler (95% AFUE): **$10,500–$15,500**
- Combi boiler with on-demand hot water: **$12,000–$17,000**
- High-efficiency gas furnace + AC ready: **$8,500–$13,500**

## Step 5 — Chimney lining

Almost every Yonkers oil-to-gas conversion needs a stainless steel chimney liner ($1,200–$2,500). Old masonry chimneys can't safely vent low-temperature gas exhaust.

## Step 6 — Final inspection and meter set

Yonkers Building Dept inspects, then Con Ed activates the meter. Total project timeline: **6–12 weeks**.

## Rebate stack for Yonkers conversions

- **Con Edison high-efficiency boiler rebate:** $400–$1,000
- **NYSEG / Con Ed conversion incentive:** up to $1,500
- **NYS Clean Heat (heat pump only):** consider going further and switching to a heat pump for $5,000–$10,000

The federal 25C tax credit is no longer part of this stack — it was terminated for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025.

## Total realistic Yonkers project cost

**$13,500–$22,000** all-in for a typical conversion, **$11,000–$19,000** after rebates.
