Mamaroneck (10543) sits between the Sound and I-95, with older housing and frequently damp basements. Indoor air quality complaints — allergies, basement smell, condensation on windows — are some of the most common service calls we get here.

## The 4 IAQ problems Mamaroneck actually has

1. **High summer humidity** (often 60–75% indoors)
2. **Basement mold and musty odors**
3. **Pollen, mold, and traffic particulate intrusion**
4. **Winter dryness from over-firing furnaces**

## Solutions ranked by impact in Mamaroneck

### 1. Whole-house dehumidifier — biggest impact

A ducted dehumidifier (Aprilaire E100, Ultra-Aire, Santa Fe) installed on the return removes **70–130 pints/day** and keeps the whole house at a target 45–50% RH.

- **Installed cost:** $2,800–$4,500
- **Best for:** Anyone in 10543 with a finished basement

### 2. Media air filter (4–5 inch)

Replaces the 1" furnace filter with a high-MERV pleated media filter that catches 95%+ of pollen, mold spores, and most particulate.

- **Installed cost:** $450–$900 (cabinet + first filter)
- **Annual filter:** $60–$110

### 3. UV-C light on the coil

Mounted in the air handler, UV-C kills mold and bacteria growing on the cold evaporator coil. Genuinely useful in Mamaroneck's humid summers; manufacturer claims about whole-house air sterilization are overhyped.

- **Installed cost:** $450–$800

### 4. ERV (energy recovery ventilator)

Brings in fresh outside air without losing heating/cooling energy. Best for tightly-sealed newer Mamaroneck homes that smell stale.

- **Installed cost:** $2,500–$4,500

### 5. Whole-house humidifier (winter only)

Steam or bypass humidifier on the furnace adds moisture in winter. Useful if you have hardwood floors gapping or family with chronic winter dry-cough.

- **Installed cost:** $750–$1,800

## What we don't recommend

- **Portable HEPA "air purifiers" sized for one room** — useless against whole-house IAQ problems
- **Ozone generators** — actively harmful, illegal as marketed in NY
- **Duct cleaning as a routine service** — only useful if you've had construction, rodents, or visible mold
