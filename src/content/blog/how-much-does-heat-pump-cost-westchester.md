**Quick answer:** In 2025, a cold-climate heat pump install in Westchester County, NY costs **$15,000–$28,000** before rebates for a typical 2,000–3,000 sq ft home. After NYS Clean Heat ($2,000–$8,000) and the federal 25C tax credit (up to $2,000), the **net cost typically lands at $9,000–$18,000**.

## 2025 installed pricing by system type

| System | Home size | Installed price | Net after rebates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ductless mini-split (2–3 zones) | 1,200–1,800 sq ft | $10,000–$16,000 | $6,500–$11,000 |
| Ductless mini-split (4–5 zones) | 1,800–2,800 sq ft | $15,000–$24,000 | $9,500–$16,000 |
| Ductless mini-split (6–8 zones) | 2,800–4,500 sq ft | $22,000–$36,000 | $14,000–$25,000 |
| Ducted air-source heat pump | 2,000–3,000 sq ft | $18,000–$28,000 | $11,000–$19,000 |
| Hybrid (heat pump + existing furnace/boiler) | Any | $14,000–$22,000 | $8,000–$15,000 |
| Geothermal (ground-source) | 2,500+ sq ft | $35,000–$60,000+ | $20,000–$40,000 |

Pricing assumes adequate electrical service. Panel upgrades add $2,500–$5,500.

## What's included in a typical Westchester install

- Outdoor condenser unit (cold-climate rated to -5°F)
- Indoor heads (ductless) or air handler (ducted)
- Refrigerant line sets, electrical, condensate
- Wall mounting, brackets, line-set covers
- Manual J load calculation
- Permit and inspection (Westchester municipality)
- NYS Clean Heat rebate paperwork
- Commissioning + AHRI documentation for tax credit
- Labor warranty (1 year standard, 2–5 years available)

## What drives the price up

- **Electrical panel upgrade** ($2,500–$5,500) — most common add-on
- **Salt-air-coated condenser** ($300–$600) — Sound Shore homes
- **Long line sets** (>50 ft) — common in older homes with detached condenser locations
- **Removing oil tank** ($800–$2,500) — for full electrification
- **Ductwork modifications** (ducted only) — $1,500–$6,000

## Rebates available in Westchester (2025)

| Program | Amount |
|---|---|
| NYS Clean Heat (whole-home) | $2,000–$8,000 |
| NYS Clean Heat (geothermal) | $1,500–$2,000 per ton |
| Federal 25C tax credit | 30% up to $2,000/yr |
| HEEHRA (income-qualified) | Up to $8,000 |
| Con Edison heat pump bonus | $200–$500 per indoor head |

Rebates apply to the contractor quote — you don't file separately for NYS Clean Heat.

## A real Westchester example

3,200 sq ft Bronxville colonial, replacing an oil boiler with a 6-zone ductless mini-split + air handler hybrid:

- Equipment + install: $24,500
- NYS Clean Heat: -$5,500
- ConEd indoor head bonus: -$1,200
- Federal 25C: -$2,000
- **Net cost: ~$15,800**
