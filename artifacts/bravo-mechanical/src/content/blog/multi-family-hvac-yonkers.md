Yonkers has more two- and three-family homes than any other Westchester city. If you own one, HVAC decisions affect tenant comfort, vacancy rates, and your operating costs in ways single-family owners never deal with.

## Common Yonkers multi-family HVAC setups

### Single boiler, multiple radiators

The 1920s–1940s standard. One boiler heats both/all units, owner pays for fuel, often included in rent. Tenants control heat with TRVs (thermostatic radiator valves) or just radiator handles.

### Per-unit furnaces

Each apartment has its own furnace and ductwork. Tenant pays gas bill. Owner responsible for equipment.

### Per-unit boilers

Each unit has its own boiler. Less common in Yonkers but increasing in renovations.

### Through-the-wall PTAC

Old apartment-style cooling. Each unit has its own. Cheap to replace but loud.

### Mini-split per apartment

Modern conversion choice. Owner installs, tenant operates.

## Owner pain points and solutions

### "Tenants run the heat to 80°F"

Switch from owner-paid to per-unit gas service if possible. Otherwise install central control with TRVs and a building thermostat with weather compensation. Cuts overheating by 25–40%.

### "I get no-heat calls every winter"

Two solutions:
1. Schedule annual fall tune-ups for every unit (deductible expense)
2. Replace 20+ year old equipment proactively, not reactively

### "AC season kills me"

Multi-family ductless mini-splits with heat-pump capability are the modern Yonkers play:
- One outdoor unit serves up to 8 indoor heads
- Each tenant gets independent control
- Heat pump qualifies for NYS Clean Heat rebates ($5,000–$10,000+ for a 2–3 family)
- Eliminates window AC liability

## Permits and inspections

Yonkers Building Department requires permits for boiler/furnace replacement and most ductless installs. We pull all permits — multi-family permit fees run $400–$900 typically.

## Rebates that apply

- **Con Edison multifamily rebate program** — up to $5,000+ per unit for heat pump retrofits
- **NYS Clean Heat multifamily** — substantial per-unit incentives
