Ardsley (10502) homes — particularly the 1950s–1970s split-levels and ranches that dominate the village — almost universally have leaky ductwork. Industry studies put typical leakage at **20–35%** of system airflow. That's hundreds of dollars per year in wasted utility bills.

## Signs of leaky ductwork in your Ardsley home

- Some rooms 5°F warmer or cooler than others (no zoning)
- Dusty rooms even after cleaning
- Basement or attic noticeably "warmed" or "cooled" by the system
- HVAC runs constantly but house never quite reaches setpoint
- High utility bills relative to neighbors
- Visible disconnected joints, gray cloth tape, or dust streaks at joints

## Where Ardsley ducts typically leak

1. **At the air handler cabinet** — manufacturer panels rarely seal well
2. **At trunk-to-branch takeoffs** — flex duct pulled loose, no mastic
3. **Boot connections (where duct meets register)** — gypsum dust falls into the gap
4. **Plenum corners** — sheet metal screws back out over time
5. **Old flex duct** — inner liner tears, outer jacket holds shape (looks fine but leaks)

## How to test (DIY-ish)

- Smoke pencil at every visible joint with system running
- Hand-feel suspected joints — leaking air feels obvious
- Pull supply registers and look at the boot-to-drywall seal

For a real number, hire a pro to do a duct blaster test (~$350–$550 in Ardsley).

## Sealing options ranked by effectiveness

### Manual mastic sealing — gold standard

A tech accesses each visible joint and applies fibrous mastic by brush. **$650–$1,500** for a typical Ardsley split-level.

### Aeroseal (sprayed in from inside)

Pressurizes the duct system with aerosolized sealant that finds and seals leaks from the inside. Great for inaccessible ducts. **$1,800–$3,500**.

### Replace flex with rigid

For homes with collapsed/torn flex duct, replacement is sometimes the only fix. **$2,500–$6,000+**.

## Rebates in Ardsley

- **Con Edison Energy Conscious Building** — partial rebates for documented duct sealing
- **NYS Comfort Home** — duct sealing qualifies as part of envelope improvement, $1,000–$3,000+ depending on scope
- Pairs perfectly with insulation upgrades for max rebate stacking

## What you'll feel after sealing

- More consistent room temperatures
- Faster recovery to setpoint
- 10–25% lower utility bills (highly variable)
- Less dust
- Quieter system (reduced static pressure)
