Port Chester (10573) homes range from 1900s wood-frame to mid-century brick to modern condos along the waterfront. Each has its own signature noises. Here's how to read them.

## Banging and water hammer (steam boilers)

**Cause:** Trapped condensate slamming into incoming steam.

**Why Port Chester:** Many pre-1950 homes have radiators that have settled out of pitch. Condensate pools instead of draining back, then steam hammers it.

**Fix:** Shim radiator legs to restore pitch toward the inlet. Replace old air vents. If banging persists in the basement piping, near-boiler piping likely needs to be re-done.

## Whistling or whooshing (forced air)

**Cause:** Restricted airflow — usually a clogged filter or closed registers.

**Quick check:** Replace filter, open all registers, listen again.

**Persistent whistling:** Undersized return ducts (extremely common in older Port Chester homes with flipped basements/finished living space).

## Clicking before ignition (gas furnace)

**Normal** if it clicks 2–3 times then ignites. **Problem** if it clicks 5+ times then locks out — that's a failing igniter or flame sensor. Usually $150–$350 to fix.

## Loud hum from outdoor unit (heat pump in winter)

**Cause:** Defrost cycle running.

**Normal:** Periodic 10-minute hum every 30–90 minutes when outdoor temps are 25–40°F.

**Problem:** Continuous hum + ice buildup means the defrost board has failed.

## Vibration from boiler or air handler

**Cause:** Loose mounting hardware, failing blower bearings, or out-of-balance blower wheel.

**Severity:** Annoying now, expensive later. Bearings rarely last more than 6 months once they start humming.

## Pop or boom on ignition

**Cause:** Delayed ignition — gas built up in the burn chamber before lighting.

**Severity:** Dangerous. Crack-the-heat-exchanger dangerous. Stop using the system and call for service.

## Knocking from radiators (hot water systems)

**Cause:** Air trapped in the radiator or system.

**Fix:** Bleed the radiator using the bleeder valve at the top.

## Hissing from a single radiator (steam)

**Cause:** Air valve doing its job (normal) — but should stop when steam reaches it.

**Problem:** Continuous loud hiss = stuck-open valve. $20 part, 5-minute fix.

- Gas smell + any noise
- Pop/boom on ignition
- Continuous outdoor unit hum with ice
- Vibration plus burning smell
