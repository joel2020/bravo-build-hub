If you live in a White Plains colonial, split-level, or pre-war multi-family and your boiler is pushing 20 years, you're already on borrowed time. Here's what a replacement actually costs in 2025 — no "starts at" gimmicks.

## Typical 2025 White Plains boiler replacement ranges

| System | Typical install range |
|---|---|
| Standard cast-iron gas boiler (82–84% AFUE) | $7,500 – $10,500 |
| High-efficiency condensing gas boiler (95%+ AFUE) | $10,500 – $15,500 |
| Oil-to-gas conversion (boiler + line + chimney work) | $14,000 – $22,000 |
| Oil boiler replacement (in-kind) | $9,500 – $14,000 |
| Combi boiler (heat + domestic hot water) | $11,000 – $16,500 |

Pricing assumes a single-family home in 10601, 10603, 10605, 10606, or 10607 with reasonable basement access.

## What pushes a White Plains job toward the high end

- **Chimney lining** — most pre-1970 White Plains homes need a stainless liner when downsizing to a high-efficiency unit.
- **Asbestos pipe insulation** — common in homes built before 1980; abatement adds $1,500–$4,000.
- **Near-boiler piping rebuilt to spec** — most "cheap" quotes skip this and the new boiler short-cycles within a year.
- **Permits and inspections** — White Plains Building Department requires permits for boiler replacement; budget $250–$500.
- **Gas line upsize** — some Battle Hill and Highlands homes have undersized gas lines for modern high-output boilers.

## Rebates that actually apply in White Plains

- **Con Edison high-efficiency boiler rebate** — $400–$1,000 depending on AFUE.
- **NYS Clean Heat** — only applies if you switch to a heat pump, not boiler-to-boiler.
- **Federal 25C tax credit** — up to $600 for a qualifying high-efficiency gas boiler.

## Should White Plains homeowners switch to a heat pump instead?

For homes with existing ductwork or open floor plans, yes — Clean Heat rebates can knock $5,000–$10,000 off a heat pump install, often making it cheaper than a new condensing boiler after incentives. For radiator homes, a high-efficiency boiler is usually still the right call.
