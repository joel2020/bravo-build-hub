If your Eastchester (10709, 10583) water heater is leaking, rusting, or older than 12 years, it's time. Here's the honest tank-vs-tankless breakdown for typical Eastchester homes.

## Tank water heater replacement

**Best for:** Most 3-bath single-family homes, anyone replacing in-kind, smaller budgets.

| Type | Installed cost | Lifespan |
|---|---|---|
| 40-gal gas | $2,200–$3,200 | 10–12 years |
| 50-gal gas | $2,500–$3,800 | 10–12 years |
| 50-gal electric | $2,000–$3,000 | 10–13 years |
| 80-gal heat pump (hybrid) | $3,800–$5,500 | 13–15 years |

## Tankless water heater replacement

**Best for:** Homes with 3+ bathrooms, simultaneous shower demand, or chronic hot-water shortages.

| Type | Installed cost | Lifespan |
|---|---|---|
| Gas tankless (Navien, Rinnai, Noritz) | $4,800–$7,500 | 18–22 years |
| Electric tankless | $2,500–$4,500 | 15–20 years |

## When tankless makes sense in Eastchester

- 3+ bathroom Tudor, colonial, or center-hall
- Family of 4+ with morning shower stacking
- Recent kitchen/bath addition that strained the old tank
- Long-term homeowner (payback period: 6–9 years on gas savings)

## When tankless is the wrong choice

- Tight basement utility closet (tankless needs combustion air + venting)
- Undersized gas line — common in 1950s Eastchester ranches; upgrade adds $800–$2,000
- Hard-water homes without a softener (tankless heat exchangers scale fast in Eastchester)
- Selling the home in <5 years

## Eastchester permits

Yes — the Town of Eastchester requires a plumbing permit for water heater replacement (~$125). We pull it; some big-box "free install" contractors don't, leaving you with a code violation at sale.

## Hybrid heat pump water heaters — the hidden winner

For Eastchester homes with a basement utility room above 50°F, a heat pump water heater cuts hot-water energy use by **60–70%** and qualifies for a $1,750 NYS Clean Heat rebate plus federal 25C credit. Often the cheapest 5-year cost of any option.
