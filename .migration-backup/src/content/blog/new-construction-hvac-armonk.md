Armonk (10504) and the Town of North Castle have some of the most complex new-construction and major-renovation projects in Westchester — large homes, energy-conscious owners, and code requirements that have tightened significantly since 2023.

## Step 1 — Manual J load calculation (non-negotiable)

Every Armonk new build needs a room-by-room ACCA Manual J load calculation. This isn't a back-of-napkin "ton per 500 sq ft" number — it's based on:
- Window area, type, orientation
- Wall construction and insulation (R-values)
- Air infiltration (target ACH50)
- Internal gains (occupants, appliances)
- Local design temps (Armonk: 5°F winter, 88°F summer at 75% humidity)

Without this, your system will be 30–50% oversized and short-cycle for its entire life.

## Step 2 — Choose the system architecture

For most new Armonk builds, the right answer is one of:

### A. Cold-climate heat pump (all-electric)

- Best for net-zero / Passive House targets
- Qualifies for $5,000–$10,000+ in NYSERDA Clean Heat rebates
- Needs ample electrical service (200A minimum, often 400A for large homes)
- Backup electric resistance for design-day extremes

### B. Dual fuel (heat pump + gas furnace)

- Heat pump above ~25°F (cheap), furnace below (warm)
- Lower operating cost than heat pump alone in Armonk's climate
- Slightly less rebate eligibility
- Best balance for 4,500+ sq ft homes

### C. Geothermal

- $40,000–$80,000+ depending on loop type and home size
- 30% federal tax credit + Clean Heat rebate
- Best for large lots (most of Armonk qualifies)
- 25–50 year equipment life

## Step 3 — Zoning

Most new Armonk builds need 3–5 zones minimum:
- Master suite (independent control, often staffed/guest schedule)
- Main living level
- Kid bedrooms
- Basement / bonus
- Pool house / detached structures

## Step 4 — Ventilation (now required)

Armonk new builds with tight envelopes (ACH50 < 3.0) require mechanical ventilation per NYS energy code. ERV (energy recovery ventilator) is the standard answer — $3,500–$6,500 installed.

## Step 5 — Permits with Town of North Castle

- Mechanical permit required
- Energy code compliance forms (ResCheck or COMcheck)
- Manual J/D/S documentation submission for systems >$10,000

## Typical new-construction HVAC spend in Armonk

- 4,000 sq ft home, dual fuel, 3 zones, ERV: **$45,000–$65,000**
- 6,000 sq ft home, geothermal, 4 zones, ERV: **$80,000–$120,000**
- 8,000+ sq ft estate, multi-equipment, 6+ zones: **$150,000+**
