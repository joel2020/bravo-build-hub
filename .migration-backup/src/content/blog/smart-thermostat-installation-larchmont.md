Larchmont (10538) homes are split roughly 50/50 between modern central HVAC and older boiler-only setups. Smart thermostat selection matters a lot — and not every model works on every Larchmont system.

## Quick compatibility check

| Your system | Recommended thermostat |
|---|---|
| Central forced air + AC | Ecobee Premium, Nest Learning |
| Heat pump (single or dual stage) | Ecobee Premium, Nest Learning |
| Boiler only (no AC) — has C-wire | Ecobee, Nest |
| Boiler only — no C-wire | Ecobee + PEK adapter, or Honeywell T6 Pro |
| Steam (one-zone) | Honeywell T6 Pro, Sensi |
| Multiple zones (zone board) | Ecobee with smart sensors |

## The C-wire problem in Larchmont

Most pre-1990 Larchmont homes were wired with only 4 conductors (R, W, Y, G) — no constant 24V "C" wire to power a smart thermostat. Three options:

1. **Run a new wire** — clean but $250–$500 in labor
2. **Use a Power Extender Kit (PEK)** included with Ecobee — works on most systems, free
3. **Choose a battery-powered model** — Honeywell Lyric or T6 Pro

## Con Edison rebates (2025)

Con Ed pays **$50–$85** for installing a qualifying ENERGY STAR smart thermostat in Larchmont (and most of Westchester). Models that qualify: Nest Learning, Nest Thermostat, Ecobee 3 Lite/Premium, Honeywell T9/T10. Rebate filed by your installer or DIY.

## Ecobee Premium — best overall for Larchmont

- Built-in Alexa
- Free remote sensors (huge for two-story Larchmont colonials with one zone)
- Best heat-pump optimization
- $250 list, often $200 on sale

## Nest Learning — best looking, slightly less flexible

- Beautiful design, learns schedule automatically
- Weaker on multi-stage heat pumps
- $280 list

## Honeywell T9 — best for boiler-only homes

- Works without a C-wire on most systems
- Includes one remote room sensor
- $200 list, frequently rebated to $115
