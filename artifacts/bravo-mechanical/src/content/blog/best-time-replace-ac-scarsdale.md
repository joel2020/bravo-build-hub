Most Scarsdale homeowners replace their AC the week it dies — usually mid-July, in 95°F heat, paying premium pricing and waiting 5–10 days for parts. There's a better way.

## The short answer: October through March

Off-season AC replacement in Scarsdale typically saves **10–20%** vs. peak summer pricing, with same-week scheduling and full rebate availability.

## Month-by-month for Scarsdale (10583)

**October–November** — Best overall window. Contractors have capacity, equipment is in stock, and you can run a full commissioning cycle before next summer. Heat pump rebates are easy to lock in.

**December–February** — Cheapest pricing of the year. Equipment manufacturers run dealer rebates. Downside: a cold-day install means refrigerant testing has to wait for warmer weather.

**March–April** — Pre-season tune-up and replacement window. Still good pricing, full inventory, and you beat the May rush.

**May–June** — Pricing rises, lead times stretch to 1–2 weeks. Acceptable if you plan ahead.

**July–August** — Worst time. Emergency pricing, parts shortages, 1–2 week waits for popular equipment. Common in Heathcote, Greenacres, and Quaker Ridge during heat waves.

**September** — Decent. Demand drops fast after Labor Day.

## Why Scarsdale specifically?

Scarsdale's older Tudor and colonial housing stock often needs custom ductwork, electrical upgrades, or refrigerant line replacement — all easier to schedule when contractors aren't slammed. Many 10583 homes also qualify for **Con Edison heat pump rebates ($1,000–$3,000)** and **NYS Clean Heat incentives** that take 2–4 weeks to process.

## When to replace vs. repair

Replace if your Scarsdale system is:
- 12+ years old and using R-22 refrigerant
- Costing $1,500+ in repairs in a single season
- Running 24/7 and still not cooling upper floors
