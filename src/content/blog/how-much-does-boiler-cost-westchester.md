**Quick answer:** In 2025, a typical boiler replacement in Westchester County, NY costs **$7,500–$15,000** installed for a like-for-like gas boiler, **$9,500–$18,000** for a high-efficiency condensing boiler, and **$14,000–$22,000** for an oil-to-gas conversion. Net cost can drop $2,000–$8,000 after NYS Clean Heat and federal 25C rebates.

## Full 2025 price breakdown

| System | Installed price | Lifespan |
|---|---|---|
| Cast-iron gas boiler (80–84% AFUE) | $7,500–$11,500 | 20–30 yrs |
| High-efficiency condensing gas (95%+ AFUE) | $9,500–$15,000 | 15–20 yrs |
| Combi boiler (heat + hot water) | $10,500–$16,500 | 15–20 yrs |
| Oil boiler in-kind replacement | $9,000–$14,000 | 20–25 yrs |
| Oil-to-gas conversion (boiler + service line) | $14,000–$22,000 | 20–25 yrs |
| Electric boiler | $4,500–$8,500 | 15–20 yrs |
> Pricing and incentive availability vary by equipment, home conditions, utility program rules, and current market costs. This guide is for planning only; final pricing requires an on-site assessment.


Pricing assumes a 2,000–3,000 sq ft Westchester home with reasonable basement access.

## What drives the price up in Westchester

- **Chimney lining** ($1,200–$2,800) — required by NYS code when downsizing or switching fuels
- **Asbestos abatement** ($800–$3,500) — common on pre-1980 boilers in Yonkers, Mount Vernon, New Rochelle
- **Spec piping** ($600–$1,500) — primary/secondary loops on high-efficiency installs
- **Permits + inspections** ($350–$700) — required in every Westchester municipality
- **Gas line upsize** ($800–$2,500) — when adding combi or higher-BTU equipment

## Rebates that lower the net cost

- **Con Edison gas equipment rebate:** $200–$700 for 95%+ AFUE
- **NYS Clean Heat (heat pump alternative):** $2,000–$8,000 if you switch to a heat pump instead
- **Federal 25C tax credit:** 30% up to $600 for qualifying gas boilers, up to $2,000 for heat pumps

## Westchester-specific pricing notes

- **Yonkers, Mount Vernon, New Rochelle:** older homes, often need chimney lining + asbestos work
- **Scarsdale, Bronxville, Larchmont:** higher labor density, occasionally tight basements
- **Northern Westchester (Bedford, Katonah, Yorktown):** propane and oil more common; conversion costs higher

## Should you replace or repair?

Replace if your boiler is:
- 18+ years old
- Costing $1,500+ in repairs in a single season
- Below 80% AFUE (your bills are ~30% higher than they should be)
- Leaking or showing cracked sections
